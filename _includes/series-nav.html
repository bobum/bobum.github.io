Jekyll excludes future-dated posts from site.posts, so parts that have not been released yet simply do not appear here. The list grows on its own as each part goes live -- no dead links, no manual editing. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series_parts = site.posts | where: "series", page.series | sort: "part" -%} {%- if series_parts.size > 0 %}
